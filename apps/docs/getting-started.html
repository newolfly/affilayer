Getting started

Four steps from zero to your first tracked click — whether you promote offers or publish them.

  1. Create an account.
    Go to affilayer.com/register and choose your role: affiliate (you promote offers and earn commission) or advertiser (you publish offers and pay for conversions).
  2. Complete onboarding.
    Tell us about yourself: profile, traffic source, verticals, GEO preferences, volume and payment profile. This is what the review team reads before approving you.
  3. Wait for approval.
    Our team reviews every application. You can check your status in the app while you wait.
  4. Start working.
    Affiliates: browse offers by vertical, country, device and payout model, apply where approval is required, then generate a tracking link. Advertisers: create your first offer — payout model, caps, GEOs and traffic rules — then approve the affiliates who want to run it.

What happens next

  • Every click on your tracking link gets a unique click ID and is recorded with country, device, referrer and your sub-parameters. See Tracking.
  • Conversions are linked to the click that produced them and recorded as pending until reviewed. See Payouts.
  • Approved commissions enter the commission ledger and become payable on a Net30 schedule by default.

Demo mode. While the Supabase backend is being connected, the web app runs in demo mode: dashboards show sample data that is clearly labelled as demo. Nothing in demo mode is real — no real offers, clicks or payouts.

Need help?

Registered users can raise a ticket from the dashboard. Everyone else: support@affilayer.com. For tracking questions, include the click ID from your link so we can look it up.
