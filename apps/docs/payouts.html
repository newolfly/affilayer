Payouts

Payouts

Commissions are earned only on conversions that are approved and not reversed. Payouts are manual and admin-approved — never automatic, never guessed.

How a commission becomes payable

  1. Conversion recorded — pending.
    A conversion arrives through the signed conversion endpoint and is recorded as pending, linked to the click that produced it.
  2. Reviewed.
    Conversions are reviewed by the network team or the advertiser, and move to approved, rejected or reversed. Every change is recorded.
  3. Entered in the ledger.
    Approved commissions are written to the append-only commission ledger — the same numbers both sides see.
  4. Payable on Net30.
    Approved commissions become payable 30 days after the end of the month in which they were approved.
  5. Payout requested and approved.
    Request a payout from the Payouts page in your dashboard when your balance is payable. A person reviews it before money moves.

Methods and minimums

Payout methods and minimum thresholds are shown in your dashboard. No fixed list is published, because methods depend on country and setup. Your payment profile is collected during onboarding.

What we do not do

  • No automatic payouts — every payout is reviewed by a person.
  • No promised earnings — performance depends on the offer, the audience and the traffic.
  • No payout totals published — the network is new and the ledger speaks for itself.

Disputes

If a conversion you expected is missing, rejected or reversed, raise a ticket from your dashboard with the click ID. Fraud signals — duplicate click IDs, bot traffic, misleading creatives — can lead to review, rejection of conversions, or account suspension.
