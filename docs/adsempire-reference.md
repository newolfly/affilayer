# AdsEmpire site map — research reference (paraphrased)

Read the AdsEmpire public site (Nov 2026) purely as a structural model for
AffiLayer's public pages. Nothing on that site was copied: all AffiLayer
headlines, body copy and brand language are written in our own words, and no
statistic, award, payout total or company history from that site is claimed
for AffiLayer (AffiLayer is new — it has none of those yet).

## Positioning (paraphrased)

- An exclusive CPA network serving two sides: affiliates and advertisers.
- Two affiliate products: direct CPA offers, and a SmartLink that bundles
  offers into one link. A separate route for advertisers.
- Header: About, Advantages, Blog, Contacts, plus Login / Sign up.
- Footer: join links; Company (About, Events, FAQ, Blog); Info (Privacy,
  Terms); social links.

## Visual direction (paraphrased)

- Dark navy background with faint geometric line art, white text.
- Orange-to-yellow gradient used for headings and pill-shaped buttons.
- Centred single-column layouts, two-card rows, dark form fields with an
  orange edge.

## Homepage structure (paraphrased)

1. Hero with a rotating-vertical headline and a short intro.
2. Two product cards, each with a join button: CPA offers (exclusive/direct,
   worldwide) and SmartLink (all offers in one link, monetise traffic).
3. An achievements marquee.
4. A contacts section with a form (Name, Email, Subject, Message).

## Advantages page (paraphrased)

Headline + intro, then seven benefit cards in this order: top payouts
(personal manager negotiates rates); a large offer catalogue across
verticals; reliable full-funnel tracking; weekly payments; wide GEO range
(50+ countries); individual approach (dedicated manager); high-quality
support. Closes with the two join cards.

## FAQ page (paraphrased)

Four groups, fourteen Q&As: General (what the network is, verticals, what
SmartLink is, how to access offers); About (how to register, approval time,
why an application is declined, when stats/payments appear, SmartLink
payout, bonuses/referral); Finance (withdrawals — Net30 default, weekly
above a volume threshold — methods, minimum payouts); Traffic (rules: no
fraud, no chat/incentive traffic; some traffic types restricted per
advertiser).

## Signup forms (paraphrased)

- Affiliate: first/last name, email, password + confirm, account type
  (individual / team / company), address, country, messenger + ID.
- Advertiser: shorter — full name, email, company, website, direct
  advertiser yes/no, vertical, additional info.

## Blog (paraphrased)

- Index with category tabs (All, AdTech Insights, News, Events) and cards
  showing category, date, author and views, plus a Featured sidebar.
- Single post page with breadcrumb, sections and share links.

## Login (paraphrased)

- Separate logins for the CPA-offers product and the SmartLink product.

## What AffiLayer adopted — and what it deliberately did not

Adopted (structure only): hero with rotating verticals; two product cards;
seven-card benefits section; four-group FAQ; blog index + post structure;
contact form fields; expanded signup fields; footer columns.

Not adopted: any sentence, headline or brand wording; the achievements
marquee (we have no achievements to show); weekly payments (our payouts are
manual, admin-approved, Net30 by default); payout minimums/methods (ours are
shown in the dashboard, not in marketing copy); any payout total, award or
founding date; two separate logins (one AffiLayer account unlocks both
products).
