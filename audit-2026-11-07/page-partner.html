Become an AffiLayer partner

Partnerships that grow the network, not the fine print.

AffiLayer grows through partners: networks, platforms, agencies and communities that want to bring their audience — or their advertisers — to a network they can audit. Partnership is a working relationship, not a badge: we agree what each side brings, and we review every application by hand.

Bring your audience — introduce affiliates or advertisers from your community or platform.
Bring your offers — advertisers and offer owners can publish through the network with their own caps and rules.
Grow together — we review every partnership application ourselves. Tell us what you have in mind.
