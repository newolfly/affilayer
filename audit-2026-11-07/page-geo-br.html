CPA affiliate network in Brazil

In Brazil: the same tracking, the same ledger, the same honest payout.

Affiliates in Brazil can join AffiLayer, apply to offers and earn commission on attributed conversions. The network is open worldwide, and each offer states the countries it accepts, so you only promote where the advertiser wants traffic.

What you get in Brazil

Full-funnel tracking — every click gets a unique click ID that stays linked to its conversion.

Direct offers and SmartLink — run offers you choose, or route mixed traffic through one link.

An auditable ledger — commissions move through pending, approved, rejected or reversed, with a record of each change.

Manual payouts, Net30 — payouts are reviewed by a person before money moves.

Questions about Brazil

Can I join AffiLayer from Brazil?

Yes. AffiLayer accepts applications from affiliates and advertisers worldwide, including Brazil. Every application is reviewed by our team, typically within 2 business days.

Which offers can I run in Brazil?

Each offer states the countries it accepts up front, so you only promote where the advertiser wants traffic. Use the offer filters by country, vertical, device and payout model to find what fits.

How do payouts work in Brazil?

Payouts are manual and admin-approved, Net30 by default — the same schedule in every country. Approved commissions enter the ledger and become payable; payout methods and minimum thresholds are shown in your dashboard.
