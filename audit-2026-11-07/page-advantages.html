What AffiLayer does differently

What we do differently is simple: you can check our work.

Most networks ask you to trust their numbers. We built AffiLayer so you can check them: auditable tracking, an append-only commission ledger, and approval flows on both sides.

Check our work — the numbers you see are the numbers in the ledger.
Approval on both sides — affiliates apply, advertisers approve, nobody runs blind.
No promised results — we promise open records, not earnings.

Why run traffic on AffiLayer

We are a new network, so we keep our promises small and our records open. Here is what we actually do for affiliates and advertisers.

Direct offers

Offers come with the payout model, caps and traffic rules stated up front. No hidden terms discovered after the click.

SmartLink routing

One link routes each visitor to an eligible offer for their country and device, using rules you can read — not a black box.

Full-funnel tracking

Every click gets a unique click ID with country, device, referrer and your sub-parameters, and stays linked to its conversion.

Reviewed payouts

Conversions move through pending, approved, rejected or reversed with a record of each change. Payouts are manual and admin-approved — never automatic, never guessed.

Wide GEO range

Offers across many countries, with per-offer GEO targeting so you only promote where the advertiser actually wants traffic.

Dedicated support

A real person answers tickets and reviews applications. New affiliates get help choosing their first offers.

Transparent reporting

Clicks, conversions, EPC and CR broken down by date, offer, country, device and source — the same numbers both sides see.
