About AffiLayer

A small network built on open books, not big promises.

AffiLayer is a new global CPA affiliate network. We are deliberately small right now: a focused offer catalogue, a tracking engine we can explain, and a team that reviews applications and conversions by hand.

Operated by MarketSync Global LLC, a registered company in New Mexico, USA.
Tracking you can audit — every click has a unique click ID linked to its conversion.
A ledger that cannot be silently rewritten — every change is recorded.

AffiLayer is operated by MarketSync Global LLC, a registered company in New Mexico, USA. Advertisers publish offers with a clear payout model and conversion definition; affiliates promote those offers and earn commission on attributed conversions.

The network is built around three principles: tracking that can be audited, a ledger that cannot be silently rewritten, and approval flows that keep both sides in control. Affiliates apply to offers, advertisers approve them, and conversions move through pending, approved, rejected or reversed states with a record of each change.

We do not run traffic ourselves, we do not buy traffic on your behalf, and we do not promise results to either side — performance depends on the offer, the audience and the traffic. What we promise is that the numbers you see are the numbers in the ledger, and that payouts are reviewed by a person before money moves.

As a new network we are growing our offer catalogue and our advertiser base offer by offer. If you want to work with us early, that is exactly the right time to join.
